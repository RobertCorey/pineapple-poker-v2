How to play

From deal to win in 6 steps

Bottom beats middle beats top — keep your rows in order or you'll foul.

Open a room

Create one for a 6‑char code + invite link, or join with a friend's code.

Get your first 5

You're dealt 5 cards. Place all five across your top, middle, and bottom rows.

Play the streets

Each turn, take 3 cards — keep 2, discard 1 — until all 13 slots are full.

Stay legal

A finished board must read bottom ≥ middle > top. Break it and you foul.

Reveal & score

+1 per row won, +3 for a scoop, plus royalties for premium hands. −6 for a foul.

Take the win

A match is 3 rounds. The highest total score wins it all.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Completely free, playable in any modern browser. No account, no download, no catch.

How many people can play?

2 to 16 players per room. Short on humans? Add AI bots to fill the table.

I've never played this kind of poker — can I jump in?

Yes! If you can recognize a pair, a flush, and a full house, you'll be fine. There's an interactive How to play guide built into the game, and a turn timer so nobody waits while you learn.

What makes Open Face Chinese different?

There's no betting and no bluffing — every card is placed in the open. The skill is in where you commit each card across your three rows, knowing you can't move it later and one misordered board fouls the whole thing. Pure decisions, no chips.

Can I play on my phone?

Absolutely — it's built mobile‑first and runs great on phones, tablets, and desktops.
