## [1.0.0] — Live

The first public release of Pineapple Poker — real‑time, multiplayer **Open Face Chinese Pineapple Poker** in your browser. Everything you need to deal in:

### 🎮 Gameplay
- The full game: build three stacked rows over five deals, score row‑by‑row against the table, and avoid the foul.
- **Full scoring** — row wins, the +3 scoop bonus, royalties for premium hands, and foul penalties — with a breakdown that shows exactly where each point came from.
- **3‑round matches** with cumulative scoring; highest total wins.

### 🤝 Multiplayer
- Real‑time rooms for **2–16 players** with 6‑character codes and **one‑click invite links**.
- **AI bots** to fill a table or practice solo.
- **Spectators** can watch a match in progress and join the next round.
- **Configurable turn timer** (5–120s) so games never stall; idle players are auto‑placed.

### 📱 Experience
- Mobile‑first design that also plays great on desktop.
- A **four‑color deck** for instant board readability.
- An interactive **How to play** guide built right into the game.
